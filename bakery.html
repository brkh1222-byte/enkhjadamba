My favourite recipe

Classic vanilla sponge

The secret ratio: butter : sugar : flour : eggs = 1 : 1 : 1 : 1 by weight. Change the number of cakes and the amounts update.

Number of cakes: 1

IngredientAmount
Butter (soft)
Sugar
Self-raising flour
Eggs
Vanilla extract
Milk
  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line two 20 cm round tins. (Ask a grown-up to help with the oven!)
  2. Beat butter and sugar until pale and fluffy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, then the milk.
  5. Split evenly between the tins (weigh them for a perfect 50% each).
  6. Bake 20–25 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool, then fill with jam and cream.

Math in the kitchen

Bigger tin, more cake

Area of a round tin is π r². A 24 cm tin vs a 20 cm tin: (12/10)² = 1.44, so you need about 44% more batter, not 20%.

8 slices, 3 cuts

Cut a round cake into 8 equal pieces with only 3 straight cuts? Two cuts across the top make 4 quarters, then one cut sideways through the middle splits each quarter into a top and bottom layer. 4 × 2 = 8.

Fractions everywhere

Half a recipe of ¾ cup sugar is ¾ × ½ = ⅜ cup. Measuring cups are a fraction workout!
