01My favourite recipe

Classic vanilla sponge

The secret is equal weights: butter, sugar, flour and eggs in the ratio 1 : 1 : 1 : 1. Change the number of cakes and every amount updates.

1 cake(s)
IngredientAmount
Butter (soft)
Sugar
Self-raising flour
Eggs
Vanilla extract
Milk

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and line two 20 cm round tins. Ask a grown-up to help with the oven!
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until pale and fluffy.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  4. Fold in the flour, then the milk.
  5. Split the batter evenly between the tins. Weigh them for a perfect 50% each.
  6. Bake 20–25 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool, then fill with jam and cream.

02Math in the kitchen

(12/10)² = 1.44

Bigger tin, more cake

A tin's area is π r². Going from a 20 cm to a 24 cm tin needs 1.44× the batter: 44% more, not 20%.

4 × 2 = 8

8 slices, 3 cuts

Two cuts across the top make 4 quarters. One cut sideways through the middle splits each into 2 layers.

3/4 × 1/2 = 3/8

Fractions everywhere

Halving a recipe with 3/4 cup of sugar means 3/8 cup. Measuring cups are a fraction workout.

180 °C

Heat & time

Too hot and the outside burns before the middle sets. Baking balances temperature and time.
